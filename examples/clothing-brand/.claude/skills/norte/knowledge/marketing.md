# Marketing knowledge for Norte

Level: professional · Example file

> This is a shortened illustration of the format. In a real run, phase 6 fills it with research on the user's actual market, and every important claim carries a link. Items here are general practice, not sourced data.

## How professionals approach this

- Plan in pillars (for example: product, culture, community) with a fixed share each, so the feed is not all sales.
- Drops follow a three-beat rhythm: teaser, reveal, last call.
- Judge a period by one business metric (sell-through, store visits from social), not by likes.

## Per agent

### norte-marketing-strategist
- Quality checklist: one metric per period; at most 1 in 4 posts is a direct sales push; every drop has teaser, reveal and last call; every piece has a brief.
- Common mistakes: calendars with no briefs; planning paid ads with no budget; copying a trend calendar without a business goal.

### norte-marketing-trend-scout
- Quality checklist: last 14 days only; a real example link per item; fit for streetwear and the 18 to 28 audience.
- Common mistakes: reporting trends that already peaked; estimating views instead of reading them.

### norte-marketing-carousel-writer
- Quality checklist: slide 1 hooks in under 8 words; one idea per slide; under 30 words per slide; product details copied from the product file; one call to action.
- Common mistakes: putting the key message in the caption only; a different call to action on every slide.

### norte-marketing-fact-checker
- Quality checklist: prices, sizes, dates match the product file exactly; every number has a source; no unproven sustainability or handmade claims.
- Common mistakes: checking only the slides and not the caption; passing "approximately right" prices.

### norte-marketing-canva-designer
- Quality checklist: text identical to the approved copy; brand template fonts and colors; readable on a phone.
- Common mistakes: shortening copy to make it fit; mixing accent colors from different drops.
