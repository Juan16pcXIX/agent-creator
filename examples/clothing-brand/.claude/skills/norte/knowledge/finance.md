## How professionals approach this

- A 13-week cash forecast, updated weekly or before any big commitment, is the standard tool for small businesses with lumpy cash cycles.
- Model cash by the date money actually moves (payout schedules, supplier terms), not by the date of the sale.
- Keep a minimum cash buffer (here: 4 weeks of fixed costs) and treat any week below it as a risk.

## Per agent

### norte-finance-bookkeeper
- Quality checklist: output total equals export total; refunds and chargebacks handled as reversals; internal transfers marked as transfers.
- Common mistakes: counting a payout from Shopify as new revenue when the sale was already recorded; guessing categories.

### norte-finance-cashflow-forecaster
- Quality checklist: assumptions listed apart from data; three sell-through scenarios per drop; payment timing by date.
- Common mistakes: one optimistic scenario only; forgetting the workshop deposit is paid weeks before the drop sells.
